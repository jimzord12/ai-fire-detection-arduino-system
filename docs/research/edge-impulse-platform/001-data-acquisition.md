# Edge Impulse Platform - Create an Impulse

## Data Acquisition Summary

- We have 300 samples in total, 49 minutes and 30 seconds of data.
- We used 80% of the data for training (240 samples) and 20% for testing (60 samples).
- We have 3 classes/labels: "fire", "no_fire", and "false_alarm".
- We have 6 features: Smoke, VOC, CO, Flame, Temperature, Humidity.
