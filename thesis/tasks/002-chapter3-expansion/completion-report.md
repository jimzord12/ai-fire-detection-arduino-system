# Completion Report: Thesis Expansion — Chapters 3, 4, 5, 10

In response to faculty feedback regarding academic depth, a systematic expansion of the thesis was undertaken across five chapters. The most significant intervention was the complete rewrite of **Chapter 3 (Literature Review and Theoretical Background)**, which grew from ~2,000 to ~4,400 words. Each sub-section was deepened with specific study findings, technical justifications, and comparative analysis: the literature strategy now documents the search queries, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and screening funnel; the evolution of sensing section now covers fusion architectures (voting, Bayesian, feature-level) and specific ML approaches (SVM, CNN, LSTM); the TinyML section now details quantization theory, pruning, knowledge distillation, and confidence calibration concerns for safety-critical deployment; and the multi-modal sensing section now addresses MEMS cross-sensitivity, environmental drift, and the JDL data fusion model. All section glossaries were also reformatted to eliminate typst compilation warnings.

Beyond Chapter 3, three additional chapters were expanded to strengthen the narrative coherence and academic rigor of the document. **Section 4.2 (Research Objectives)** was expanded from a bare bullet list (~245 words) into a structured presentation (~740 words) where each of the six objectives is accompanied by a rationale grounded in the preceding literature. **Sections 5.4.2 and 5.4.3** (Chapter 5 Literature Review) were deepened from ~500 combined words to ~1,140 words, adding discussion of early multi-gas array research, IR detection vulnerabilities, rule-based vs. data-driven fusion comparison, and the TinyML deployment pipeline. **Section 10.3 (Contributions to the Field)** was expanded from ~260 to ~570 words, adding two new contributions—the three-class classification paradigm and the hybrid decision architecture—alongside deeper discussion of the existing contributions.

In total, approximately 3,800 words of substantive academic content were added across four chapters, all supported by the existing bibliography of 66 peer-reviewed entries. The thesis compiles cleanly via `typst compile main.typ` with zero content warnings, and all previously identified reference placeholders have been flagged in the project-level `TODO.md` for resolution upon repository creation.
