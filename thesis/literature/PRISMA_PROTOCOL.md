# Literature Review Selection Strategy

**Goal**: Identify relevant studies on multi-sensor fire detection, sensor fusion, and TinyML deployment (2015–present).

## 1. Core Selection Criteria

- **Timeframe**: 2015 – Present.
- **Topics**:
  - Fire/Smoke detection (Indoor & Wildland).
  - Sensor fusion (Kalman, Bayesian, Neural Networks).
  - TinyML/Embedded AI for microcontrollers.
  - False alarm/nuisance alarm reduction strategies.
- **Evidence**: Must include experimental results or a defined methodology.

## 2. Information Sources

- **Primary**: IEEE Xplore, Google Scholar.
- **Secondary**: ScienceDirect, MDPI (Sensors), and manufacturer datasheets (for hardware).

## 3. Simple Search Strategy

Search for combinations of:

- `("fire detection" OR "smoke detection" OR "wildfire")`
- `AND ("sensor fusion" OR "TinyML" OR "microcontroller")`
- `AND ("false alarm" OR "nuisance alarm")`

## 4. Screening & Data Extraction

1. **Scan**: Filter by title and abstract for relevance to "multi-modal" and "low-power".
2. **Review**: Read full texts to extract:
   - Sensors used (e.g., Gas, IR, Thermal).
   - Fusion Method.
   - Target Hardware (e.g., Arduino, ESP32, STM32).
   - How they handled false alarms.
