# Research Query: 9.1 Ethical and Safety Aspects of AI in Life-Critical Systems

Research the ethical, legal, and safety implications of deploying AI-driven fire detection systems.

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Why is "Black Box" AI a concern in life-safety systems? Research methods for making TinyML decisions interpretable for fire investigators.
2. **Liability and Certification**: Who is responsible if an AI-based detector fails? Research the current legal landscape regarding AI in safety-critical infrastructure.
3. **Reliability vs. Innovation**: Contrast the rigorous testing required for traditional "dumb" sensors with the rapid iteration of ML models.
4. **Data Privacy**: Research the implications of "always-on" environmental monitoring in private residences.

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
