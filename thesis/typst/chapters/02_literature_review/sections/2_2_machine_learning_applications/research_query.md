# Research Query: 2.2 Machine Learning Applications in Fire Detection

Conduct a deep dive into the specific algorithms and architectures used for fire detection in the last decade.

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **Algorithm Comparison**: Compare the performance (Accuracy, F1-Score, Latency) of Random Forests, SVMs, and Neural Networks (CNNs/RNNs) for sensor-based fire detection.
2. **Feature Engineering vs. Deep Learning**: Research the effectiveness of manual feature extraction (Statistical, FFT) vs. automated feature learning in TinyML contexts.
3. **Handling Imbalanced Data**: How do researchers address the scarcity of real fire data compared to ambient data? (e.g., SMOTE, GANs, or synthetic data generation).
4. **False Alarm Mitigation**: Specifically look for ML strategies designed to distinguish between "fire smoke" and "nuisance smoke" (cooking/steam).

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
