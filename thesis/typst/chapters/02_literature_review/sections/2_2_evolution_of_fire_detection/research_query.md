# Research Query: 2.1 Evolution of Fire Detection Technologies

Research the historical and technological transition from traditional fire detection to modern intelligent systems.

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **Generational Shifts**: Trace the evolution from mechanical/thermal triggers to ionization/photoelectric sensors, and finally to MEMS-based multi-gas sensing.
2. **Integration of AI**: Identify the milestone papers (2015-2025) that introduced Machine Learning (ML) and Deep Learning (DL) into the fire detection pipeline.
3. **The Rise of TinyML**: Research the emergence of "Edge AI" in fire safety—moving from cloud-based processing to on-device inference for reduced latency and increased privacy.
4. **Standardization Trends**: How have standards like EN 54 and NFPA 72 adapted to include "multi-criteria" detectors?

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
