# Research Query: 3.1 Physics and Operating Principles of Fire-Relevant Sensors

Research the underlying physics and chemical transduction mechanisms for the sensors used in this project.

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **MEMS MOx Gas Sensors (Smoke, VOC, CO)**: Explain the Chemiresistive effect. How do Metal Oxide Semiconductors (like SnO2) interact with reducing and oxidizing gases? Research the effect of temperature on sensor sensitivity and selectivity.
2. **IR Flame Detection**: Research the "CO2 Spike" at 4.3µm and the flicker frequency (1-30Hz) of real flames. How do silicon-based IR sensors distinguish between a fire and a steady heat source?
3. **AHT20 (Capacitive Humidity/Thermal)**: Explain the physics of capacitive humidity sensing and the impact of rapid temperature/humidity fluctuations during combustion.
4. **Sensor Aging and Drift**: Research the long-term stability of MEMS gas sensors and common compensation techniques.

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
