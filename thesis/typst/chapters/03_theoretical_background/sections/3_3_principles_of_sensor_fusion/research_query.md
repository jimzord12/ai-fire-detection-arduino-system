# Research Query: 3.3 Principles of Sensor Fusion for Discrimination

Research the mathematical and architectural frameworks for multi-sensor fusion in the context of fire detection.

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **Fusion Levels**: Define and compare Data-level, Feature-level, and Decision-level fusion. Which is most suitable for low-latency TinyML applications?
2. **Bayesian and Probabilistic Methods**: Research the use of Bayesian Networks or Kalman Filters for combining gas and thermal data to reduce uncertainty.
3. **Neural Network Fusion**: How do multi-input neural networks (e.g., concatenating different sensor streams) improve classification robustness?
4. **Conflict Resolution**: How does the system handle conflicting sensor data (e.g., high heat but no CO)?

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
