# Research Query: 1.3 Research Questions

Research the state-of-the-art benchmarks for intelligent fire detection systems to provide a baseline for the following research questions:

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **Latency Benchmarks**: What are the industry and academic standards for "real-time" fire detection? Find studies reporting inference latency for TinyML models on ARM Cortex-M4 or similar microcontrollers.
2. **Sensor Fusion Efficacy**: Gather comparative data on the accuracy and false-alarm-rate (FAR) of single-sensor systems vs. multi-sensor fusion (Smoke + CO + VOC + Flame).
3. **Three-Class Classification**: Search for existing implementations of three-class fire detection (Fire, No-Fire, False Alarm). How do they quantify the reduction in false positives compared to binary models?
4. **Edge Deployment Feasibility**: Research the typical memory footprint (RAM/Flash) and power consumption of quantized neural networks used in environmental monitoring.

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
