# Research Query: 1.2 Problem Statement

Investigate the specific technical and environmental factors that lead to fire detection failure and false alarm triggers in modern smart buildings.

**Key Research Areas:**

1. **Non-Fire Nuisance Sources**: Identify the chemical and physical signatures of common false alarm triggers: cooking fumes (oil/protein combustion), water vapor (steam), aerosol sprays (VOCs), and intense IR interference (sunlight/welding).
2. **Sensor Cross-Sensitivity**: Research the limitations of MEMS gas sensors (MOx) and IR flame sensors regarding cross-sensitivity to non-target gases and environmental noise.
3. **Edge Computing Constraints**: Analyze the challenges of implementing complex sensor fusion algorithms on low-power microcontrollers (e.g., RAM/Flash limits of Cortex-M4).
4. **The "Three-Class" Necessity**: Find evidence or arguments supporting why binary classification (fire/no-fire) is insufficient for high-reliability environments compared to explicit "false alarm" class recognition.

**Requirements:**

- Provide all citations in-text.
- Include a full reference list in **APA 7th Edition**.
- Focus on sources from **2015-present**.
