**Edge Node**: A computing element that performs data processing at the "edge" of the network, near the source of the data, to reduce latency and bandwidth use.
**Nuisance Alarm**: A false alarm triggered by non-hazardous environmental factors (e.g., steam, dust, or cooking) that mimics fire signatures.
**Sensor Fusion**: The process of combining data from multiple sensors to produce information that is more accurate or complete than that provided by individual sources.
**TinyML**: A field of machine learning focused on developing models that can run on low-power, resource-constrained hardware like microcontrollers.
